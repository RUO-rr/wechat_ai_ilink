# Professional Photo Rules

## Decision

- Photo guidance is auxiliary. Use this file only when the user asks for resume/profile
  photo advice together with a resume task.
- Ask target market and use case before recommending a photo.
- Chinese domestic resumes may include a photo when requested.
- English/international resumes omit photos by default.
- If the user supplies their own photo and requests editing, use image editing.
- If no personal photo is supplied, do not generate a specific fake person; request an
  upload and provide a reusable prompt.

## Default Appearance

- face camera directly;
- center head and visible shoulders;
- white or near-white plain background;
- even natural light without harsh shadows;
- neutral, natural expression;
- simple business attire or dark plain top;
- preserve real facial features, face shape, skin tone, age, hairstyle identity, and
  other identity characteristics;
- avoid heavy retouching, structural face changes, exaggerated makeup, artistic
  stylization, or commercial glamour treatment.

## Supported Styles

- American-style registration/job photo;
- business headshot;
- academic conference headshot;
- LinkedIn professional headshot.

Use prompts in `assets/photo_prompts/`.

## Required Notice

State that AI-generated or edited images are suitable for job applications,
professional profiles, or internal display. They must not be represented as valid
passport, visa, government ID, or other official identity-document photos.
