# Experienced Candidate and Career Changer Rules

## Experienced Candidates

- Use reverse chronology.
- Prioritize recent, relevant, high-impact experience.
- For each role show title, organization, dates, scope, responsibilities, and outcomes.
- Emphasize target skills, business/research results, tools, collaboration, leadership,
  quality, compliance, scale, cost, speed, adoption, or decision impact.
- Reduce early or unrelated roles to preserve space.
- Do not expose confidential details; generalize scale or product names when needed.

## Career Changers

Identify transferable capabilities:

- project and stakeholder management;
- data analysis and decision support;
- experimental design and problem solving;
- communication and cross-functional collaboration;
- industry/domain understanding;
- tools, platforms, standards, and learning evidence.

Use direct evidence first, adjacent evidence second, transferable evidence third.
Never relabel unrelated experience as direct experience.

Add a targeted summary only when it bridges the transition with evidence. Avoid
apologetic explanations or generic claims about being a fast learner.
